VoiceServe: Progress Report

12 November 2026 · Update to the report of 5 November

Since the last report, the voice assistant has grown from answering only attendance questions to answering five kinds of student questions: attendance, fees, timetable, exams and assignments. It now gives a specific answer to each question, not a general one. Milestone 4 is one third done.

What VoiceServe is

A voice assistant inside a university student portal. A student taps a mic button on any page and asks in plain speech (or types). It answers out loud and shows a card on screen. It uses our own practice portal, Univra, filled with made-up students: same layout and menus as the real portal, our own name, logo and colours. No real student data is used.

New since the last report

The assistant can now answer these, each with a spoken reply and an on-screen card (real examples from our demo student, Aarav):

"How much fee do I owe?"You have 23,500 rupees due in total. The next payment, Hostel Fee (2nd instalment), is due on Sunday, 22 November.

"In which subject do I have least attendance?"Your lowest attendance is in Computer Networks, at 73 percent. Attend the next 3 classes in a row to reach 75.
  • Understands days and times: "today", "tomorrow", "on Friday", "next Monday", "after 2 pm", "next class".
  • Specific answers: a question about one fee, one course or the weakest subject gets an answer about exactly that, not a general summary.
  • Fixed from our own testing: we asked it real questions, found two that gave generic answers, and fixed both.

How we keep it safe and correct

  • No made-up numbers. Every amount, date and percentage comes straight from the portal's database. The AI never invents them.
  • Fake data only, and a student can only see their own data.
  • Honest when it can't: if the data can't be reached, it says so instead of guessing.
  • Free to run: everything so far works on free services; a $5-a-day spending cap protects any paid service.
