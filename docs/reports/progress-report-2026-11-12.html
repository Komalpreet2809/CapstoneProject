VoiceServe: Weekly Update

Week of 5 to 12 November 2026 · Last week we showed Milestones 1 to 3

This week we started Milestone 4 (the full assistant) and finished its first part. The assistant now answers five kinds of questions instead of one: attendance, fees, timetable, exams and assignments. Each question gets a specific answer, by voice or by typing.

1. Completed this week

Four new things the assistant can answer (real answers from our demo student, Aarav):

Student asksAssistant answers
"How much fee do I owe?"You have 23,500 rupees due in total. The next payment, Hostel Fee (2nd instalment), is due on Sunday, 22 November.
"Any classes after 2 pm on Monday?"You have 1 class on Monday, 16 November after 2 PM: CSE 343, Software Engineering, at 4 PM.
"When is my next exam?"The exam, its date and time, and the seat: R1-S2 in room A-101, Academic Block A.
"Do I have any pending assignments?"You have 4 pending assignments. The next one is Case Study 2 for Computer Networks, due Sunday.

Smarter, more specific answers:

Student asksAssistant answers
"What's my hostel fee?"Your Hostel Fee of 85,000 rupees is fully paid. Your Hostel Fee (2nd instalment) has 20,000 rupees pending.
"In which subject do I have least attendance?"Your lowest attendance is in Computer Networks, at 73 percent. Attend the next 3 classes in a row to reach 75.
"What's my next class?"Your next class is CSE 305, Database Management Systems, at 12 PM in room B3-416.
  • Understands days and times: "today", "tomorrow", "on Friday", "next Monday", "after 2 pm", "next class".
  • Two bugs found and fixed by testing it ourselves: "What's my hostel fee?" used to repeat the total fee, and "Which subject has least attendance?" used to fail. Both now give the right answer.
  • Same safety rules as before: every amount, date and percentage comes from the database, never invented by AI.
  • Public demo link updated, and all code backed up on GitHub.
